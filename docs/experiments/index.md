# Experiments

Sample experiments supported on Deep Radio:

<div class="bw-related" markdown>

- **Cellular monitoring:** live 2G, 3G and 4G spectrum and band monitoring; live monitoring of GSM and 4G LTE stations; capturing, visualising and processing GSM and LTE signals
- **Modems:** [over-the-air transmission and reception using BPSK and QPSK modems](qpsk-text-link.md); OFDM transmission and analysis; live AM and FM transmission and reception
- **Channels & signals:** verification of AWGN and small-scale fading channel distributions; verification of autocorrelation properties
- **Spectrum:** spectrum analysis from 100 MHz to 6 GHz; energy-detection-based spectrum sensing; jammer for cellular and Wi-Fi channels
- **Machine learning:** ML-based spectrum activity prediction; ML/DL-based modulation classification (BPSK, QPSK, GMSK, 16QAM, 64QAM and more)
- **5G:** 5G signal generation and transmission

</div>
